How these estimates work & sources

Rates checked on 15 October 2026. Verified payroll years: 2025/26 and 2026/27. Rates do not update automatically; future years must be verified before they are added.

Each calendar month is assumed to be paid in that month, on or after 6 April for the April payment. Income tax annualises each month's taxable pay and divides the annual result by 12, using the standard personal allowance and its taper above £100,000. This is a planning estimate, not cumulative PAYE. Year totals sum those monthly estimates; uneven earnings can produce different actual tax.

NI uses monthly thresholds and the selected category. Categories M and H require eligibility throughout the modelled period; change category if eligibility changes. Pension participation is selected manually. Qualifying earnings use the monthly £520–£4,189 band. The employee percentage is the gross pension contribution, including basic tax relief under relief at source. Additional higher or Scottish rate relief is not included. Salary sacrifice uses pre-sacrifice pay as the pension basis and assumes no employer NI saving is added to the pension.

Bank holidays use a bundled GOV.UK snapshot. They are paid only on scheduled working days unless overridden. Annual leave and bank holiday hours are separate, so changing work hours does not silently book leave. Leave totals include all planned dates in this April–March leave year. Reset only clears overrides in the selected month. Updating the weekly pattern does not change the leave budget automatically.

Estimates exclude student loans, non-standard tax codes, other income, directors' NI, statutory sick or family pay, overtime premiums, Employment Allowance and other employer reliefs. Contractual holiday pay and salary sacrifice eligibility need separate checks. This tool does not submit payroll to HMRC.

Data is saved in this browser on this device, without an account or server sync. Use a JSON backup to move it elsewhere. CSV exports contain summaries and cannot restore your calendar.
